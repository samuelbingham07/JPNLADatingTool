How it works

  1. Visual model. Each photo is embedded with a frozen open-source image encoder (CLIP ViT-B/32). A decade classifier I trained on dated listings ( photos) then scores those embeddings.
  2. Tag reading. On-device OCR reads the tag text: country of origin, RN/WPL numbers, union names, fiber content, care copy, zipper brands. You can correct the text, or tick things OCR can't see, like a missing care label.
  3. Constraint engine. Each match maps to a rule in a hand-built knowledge base: the 1971 FTC care-label rule, the Textile Fiber Act, RN registration ranges, union merger dates, countries that no longer exist. Hard bounds rule out impossible years, and soft anchors reweight likely ones.
  4. Range. The tightest decade split (early, mid or late decade, or the full decade) holding at least 55% of the probability. If nothing does, it returns a wide range and says what photos would help.
